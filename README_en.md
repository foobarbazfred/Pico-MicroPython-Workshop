# Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W and MicroPython Training Materials

## Seminar Overview
- Using MicroPython and the Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W, participants learn how to control sensors and peripheral devices and how to prototype simple systems.
- This training is not lecture-based only; participants will actually build circuits using breadboards, microcontrollers, and sensors.
- The course consists of a two-day program:
  - Day 1 is the fundamentals track, where participants learn about MicroPython, how to connect sensors and other components, and how to create simple sensor applications.
  - Day 2 is the practical track, where teams work together to design and implement an application from concept to completion with guidance and a casual "study session" style format.
    - First, decide what kind of product or system you want to create, then give a brief presentation covering what you want to improve, what seems challenging, and any key ideas.
    - Team members divide responsibilities and collaborate to build the application. A shared drive will be provided for documentation and file sharing (implementation time is planned to be about two hours).
    - If you need support, we will help as much as possible. If you cannot find a solution yourself, we will provide hints and guidance.
    - After completion, each team will present their results and share what they improved, what was difficult, what they learned, and what was interesting.

## What This Seminar Will Teach
- Microcontroller control with MicroPython
- Prototyping methods that combine microcontrollers and sensors
- Prototype development methods relevant to IoT systems
- Ideas for creating teaching materials using microcontrollers, MicroPython, and sensors
